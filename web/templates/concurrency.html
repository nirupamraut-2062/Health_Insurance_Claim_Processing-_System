Where ClaimSure uses this. Claim status changes and decisions use optimistic locking: every claim carries a version, and each form posts the version you saw. If someone else changed the claim meanwhile, your action is refused instead of overwriting theirs.
Policy cover is always changed with an atomic, guarded $inc (available_cover: {$gte: amount}), so it can never go negative or lose a payment.
Settlement runs inside session.with_transaction(): if two payments hit the same policy at once, MongoDB detects the write conflict, aborts one transaction and re-runs it.

Uses sandbox collections lab_cover and lab_payments; real claims are not touched.
