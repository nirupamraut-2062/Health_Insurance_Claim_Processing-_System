Design patterns used

Embedding (one-to-few). A claim embeds its bill, diagnosis list, documents, queries, status history and adjudication. One read loads the whole case file, and one atomic update changes status and history together.
Extended reference. Claims store member_id / hospital_id and a snapshot of the names, age, city and tier at admission time. Lists render without $lookup, and history stays correct if the master record changes later.
Subset pattern. A policy embeds just the insured members' name, relation, DOB and declared conditions — everything adjudication needs — while full KYC and contact details live in members.
Computed pattern. tat_days, settlement_tat_days, available_cover and the fraud score are computed on write, so dashboards don't recompute them on every read.
Bucketed history. renewal_history[] holds one small entry per policy year (bounded: one per year), giving premium-earned analytics without a separate collection.
Sequences & business keys. A counters collection with an atomic $inc issues claim, payment and grievance numbers. Unique indexes guarantee one payment per claim.
